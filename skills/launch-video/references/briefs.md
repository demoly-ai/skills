# Launch video brief examples

All products below are fictional.

## Good: specific lead and ending

Product: Ledgerly, invoicing for freelancers.

```text
For freelancers who hate chasing payments. Open with automatic payment reminders, then show recurring invoices and the client portal. Keep the tone friendly and plain. End by inviting viewers to send their first invoice.
```

Why it works: it names the audience, one lead feature, two supporting points in order, a tone, and a call to action that the page supports.

## Good: technical audience

Product: Tracewell, open-source tracing for Node.js services.

```text
For backend engineers. Lead with the one-line install, then show the flame graph and the slow-query alerts. Mention that it is open source. Technical, no hype.
```

## Weak, and how to fix it

```text
Make an amazing video that shows all our features and goes viral. Say we have 10,000 users.
```

Problems: no lead or audience, "all features" makes a crowded script, and the user count is not on the page. Ask which feature matters most and who the viewer is, and leave out numbers the page does not support unless the user confirms them.
